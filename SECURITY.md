# Security

Found a vulnerability in something we build or run? Tell us privately at
[whocaressoftware@gmail.com](mailto:whocaressoftware@gmail.com), with
"Security" in the subject. Please do not open a public issue.

Include what is affected, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do.
We will confirm we received it and keep you updated until it is resolved.

This policy applies to every WhoCaresSoftware repository that does not have
its own `SECURITY.md`.
