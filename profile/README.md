**Most software problems are work problems.** We start there. Then we build what's missing.

Who Cares Software is an independent software studio in Cyprus. Two founders, no account managers: you talk to the people who build it.

- **Custom software.** Internal tools and customer applications shaped around how your business runs.
- **Automation & AI.** Connect your tools, cut the repetitive work. AI where it earns its place, and nowhere else.
- **Cloud & reliability.** Infrastructure that is easy to run, update and maintain. Boring is the goal.

Most of what we build is client work, so most of this organization is private. That's the point.
Some of it we can show: [a worksite ERP](https://whocaressoftware.com/work/aigistos/), [a roguelite deck-builder](https://whocaressoftware.com/work/hex-mythica/) and [a private LLM gateway](https://whocaressoftware.com/work/private-llm-gateway/).
